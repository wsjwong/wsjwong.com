## How it Started?

Every product starts with a problem, and [AI for Sheets](https://simplemetrics.xyz?utm_source=wsjwong.com) is no exception. As someone who works extensively with data and content, I found myself repeatedly using ChatGPT to generate text for various topics. The results were impressive, but there was a significant limitation: I needed to do this for 50 different topics, and the manual process was becoming tedious.

## From Spreadsheet to Product

The solution seemed straightforward at first: create a spreadsheet, list all the topics, and use a formula to automate the text generation process. I tested this approach, and it worked surprisingly well. The efficiency gain was immediate, and the quality remained consistent across all topics.

But then came the realization: I can’t be the only one facing this challenge. If this solution was valuable to me, it could be valuable to others as well. That’s when [AI for Sheets](https://simplemetrics.xyz?utm_source=wsjwong.com) began to take shape as a product rather than just a personal tool.

## Founder Journey

I aim to create something genuinely useful and easy to use. As the founder of Simple Metrics, I use this blog to document:

- Technical challenges and solutions
- Product decisions and their outcomes
- Growth strategies and metrics
- Lessons learnt along the way

I believe in transparency and feedback. By sharing my journey, I hope to not only build a better product but also connect with people.
