## Informational Apps and Entertainment Apps

Informational Apps (AI Eats This): News, Travel Planning, Language Learning.
Entertainment Apps: TikTok, Games.

## Informational — AI Territory

AI is already doing the heavy lifting in creating informational content. Think news summaries, blog posts, SEO articles, basically anything that’s structured, data-heavy, and predictable.

Examples:

- ChatGPT — Writes research reports, SEO content, and even entire eBooks.
- [Simple Metrics AI for Sheets](https://simplemetrics.xyz?utm_source=wsjwong.com), Yep, my own app. It cranks out data analysis and data entry perfectly.

AI’s fast and cost-effective. But it’s also pretty soulless. That’s why entertainment content is still mostly human-driven.

## Entertainment — Human-Driven (For Now)

AI can generate memes, social media captions, and basic videos. But truly engaging content? That still needs a human touch. Entertainment is all about emotion and context.

We can produce content faster with AI, but the entertainment is still needed from human.

## Final Thought

AI is clearly eating up the informational space. It’s efficient, consistent, and cost-effective. But in the entertainment space, human creativity still wins.

For now.
