Recently I had an unpleasant experience purchasing a `.xyz` domain through Namecheap. The moment I completed the purchase, the domain was flagged as “reported abuse” and immediately suspended, even though I had not used it in any way.

I submitted a “Request Review”, but received no response for days. When I contacted support, they told me the issue was handled by the registry and not something Namecheap could assist with. My request for a refund was also denied.

![Namecheap .xyz domain reported abuse suspension](/blog-images/namecheap-xyz-domain-suspension.jpeg)

The entire process lacked transparency and accountability. My view is simple: if a domain is not usable, it should not be sold in the first place.

If you're considering buying a `.xyz` domain at Namecheap, I recommend being cautious to avoid the same frustration.
